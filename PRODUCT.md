# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Researchers are the primary users. Advocates, policymakers, journalists, students, and the wider public may also use the site to find credible material on bonded labour in Nepal.

## Product Purpose

UNBonded Evidence Hub is a public, source-led repository for finding articles, surveys, reports, policy documents, and related evidence on bonded labour, rehabilitation, and post-liberation vulnerability in Nepal. Success means users can quickly discover relevant sources and understand their scope before following up with the original material.

## Positioning

The hub combines a curated, coded source catalogue with transparent record-level context: systems, themes, methods, years, geography, findings, limitations, and original-source links where available.

## Operating Context

Users explore a public website through keyword search, catalogue filters, timelines, geographic coverage, methodology guidance, source-record pages, and organisation contact information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fast, straightforward discovery of articles and other evidence is non-negotiable.
- Users must be able to search and filter records by relevant criteria, including system, method, publication year, theme, geography, and source links where recorded.
- The repository is a curated research aid, not a systematic review, causal evaluation, or replacement for original sources.
- The application is a static React and Vite web app with no backend, database, or environment configuration.

## Brand Commitments

The established product name is UNBonded Evidence Hub. The current repository content is source-led and careful about uncertainty, scope, and unsupported inference.

## Evidence on Hand

- Curated source, claims, timeline, and district data: `data/sources.js`
- District map geometry: `data/nepal-districts.js`
- Organisation contact records: `data/contacts.js`
- Existing fieldwork and catalogue imagery: `assets/`
- No user testimonials, outcome benchmarks, or independent impact claims are available and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Make relevant evidence easy to find.
2. Preserve the context needed to interpret a source responsibly.
3. Make uncertainty, gaps, and incomplete metadata visible.
4. Support a broad public audience while prioritising researcher workflows.
5. Direct substantive claims back to original sources.
